Questions

Is my Canvas link safe?

Your calendar link works like a password, so it's treated like one. It's never shown back to you, written to logs, or shared.

Will this raise my grades?

We can't promise that, and we won't. It helps you start on time and shows study methods that research suggests help on average.

Does it work with my school?

If your Canvas has a Calendar Feed link at the bottom of the Calendar page, it should. Other platforms may come later.

Who is it for?

College students aged 18 and over.
